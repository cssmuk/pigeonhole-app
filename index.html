Pigeonhole

A personal email organizer that runs entirely on its owner’s own computer.

Pigeonhole sorts read mail into folders by sender, moves mail from senders the owner has chosen to a junk folder, and archives past years into encrypted files on the owner’s computer.

  • It is a private tool for one person’s own mailboxes. It is not offered to the public.
  • All processing happens on the owner’s computer. Mail is never sent to the developer, to any AI service, or to anyone else.
  • It never sends mail and never permanently deletes mail.
